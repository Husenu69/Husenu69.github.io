Frequently asked questions

Why is my oven not heating?

Common causes include a failed bake element or igniter, a faulty temperature sensor or a control problem. A technician has to inspect the oven to confirm which one.

Why does my stove burner keep clicking?

Constant clicking often points to a dirty or wet burner, a misaligned burner cap or a faulty igniter or switch. If you smell gas, leave the home and call your gas provider or 911.
