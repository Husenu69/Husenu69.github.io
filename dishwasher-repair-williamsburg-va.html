Frequently asked questions

Why is my dishwasher not draining?

A clogged filter, a blocked drain hose or a failed drain pump are the usual causes. Cleaning the filter at the bottom of the tub is a simple first check.

Why are my dishes still dirty after a cycle?

Causes include a clogged spray arm, a dirty filter, low water supply or a failed pump or heating component. We find the cause during the service call.
