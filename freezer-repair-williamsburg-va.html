Frequently asked questions

Why is there so much frost in my freezer?

Heavy frost usually comes from a worn door seal letting in warm air or a defrost-system fault. We check the seal, the defrost components and the controls.

Why is my freezer not freezing?

Possible causes include a failed fan, a defrost problem, a faulty thermostat or a sealed-system issue. The cause is confirmed by inspecting the unit.
